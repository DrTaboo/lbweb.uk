Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains the data handling of users who access my personal portfolio, lbweb.uk. I value the privacy of internet users and have therefore kept data collection to a minimum.

Last Updated: 31st of October, 2026

1) Who I am

This website is ran by "Lewis ███████" ("I", "me"), who is the data controller for the personal data described below. You can contact me via email at contactme@lbweb.uk

2) Analytics

I use Umami Analytics which is privacy focused and open-sourced to understand how the site is used. I use a self-hosted instance of Umami Analytics meaning any analytical data isn't sent to any third-party analytics provider. It records basic usage information such as pages viewed, referral source, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, approximate location (country, region and city, worked out from your IP address and not stored with it), page load performance, and visit count and duration.
Umami Analytics is configured so that:
ㅤㅤ• it does not use cookies,
ㅤㅤ• it does not store your IP address,
ㅤㅤ• it does not track you across other websites, and
ㅤㅤ• it does not build profiles of individual visitors or try to identify you.
Your IP address and browser details are briefly processed when your browser makes a request, in order to produce an anonymous, non-reversible identifier used to count visits. The raw IP address is then discarded.

My legal basis is legitimate interests (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): understanding traffic and improving the site. Analytics data is kept until I choose to delete it.

3) Hosting, Cloudflare and server logs

The site is served from my own server. Traffic passes through Cloudflare, which provides DNS, caching and security protection. Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, and may keep its own logs. Cloudflare is a US company, so this may involve transfers of data outside the UK, which Cloudflare covers with safeguards described in its own policy.

My web server (Caddy) does not keep access logs for this site.

My legal basis is legitimate interests: keeping the site secure and working.

4) Likes and view counts

The view counts and "likes" shown on posts come from my own Umami instance (see section 2); no third-party service is involved. View counts are the same anonymous page view totals described above. A "like" is recorded as an anonymous Umami event, with the same limited information as any other visit, and only totals per page are displayed publicly. When you click "like", your browser also saves a small marker in its local storage so the button remembers that you already liked that page. This stays on your device, and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings (doing so lets you like the page again).

My legal basis is legitimate interests: showing popularity statistics on my pages. Like and view data is kept for the same period as other analytics data (see section 2).

5) Cookies and local storage

I do not set any cookies for tracking or advertising. When you use the light/dark appearance switch or the "like" button (see section 4), the site saves that choice in your browser's local storage so it can remember it on your next visit. This is only done when you use those features, it is not used to track you, and it is not sent to me. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie in some circumstances (for example to detect bots). I do not load third-party advertising, social media widgets or embedded videos.

6) Contacting me

If you email me, I will use your email address and message only to reply to you. I do not share it or add you to any mailing list. Emails are automatically deleted 18 months after they arrive, unless I have deliberately marked one to keep because it relates to an ongoing collaboration, a permission or licence, or a legal reason. Deleted emails may remain in server backups for a short time until those backups are overwritten.

My legal basis is legitimate interests, or taking steps at your request if you are asking me something.

7) Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data I hold about you, to restrict or object to how I use it, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise any of these, email me at the address contactme@lbweb.uk. I will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

8) Children

This site is not aimed at children, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9) Photographs of people

Some photographs on this site show people who were in public places or at public events. I publish these as part of my creative and artistic work, and not for commercial use. I do not knowingly publish photographs that clearly identify children, or that show people in vulnerable or private moments, and I do not name individuals or reveal sensitive information about them.

If you appear in a photograph and would like it removed or blurred, email me at contactme@lbweb.uk with a link to the page or a description of the photo. I will review your request and, where reasonable, take the photo down or edit it, normally within one month and usually much sooner.

10) External links

Links to other websites are outside my control. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies.

11) Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
